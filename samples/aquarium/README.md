# Pretty Fish Aquarium recordings

**[Open the live aquarium →](https://pretty-fish-aquarium.frankmarsman.chatgpt.site)**

Six recordings of the running 3D aquarium, captured directly from its WebGL
canvas. Each lighting setting is shown with **18 and 36 fish**, at **1.0× pace**.
Both school sizes reuse eight painted fish models, with rounded bodies,
mirrored sides, flexible spines and moving fins.

**Daylight** gives a clear view of the fish. **The deep** lights the foreground
while distant fish fade into darkness. **Moon pool** uses cool nighttime light.

The GIFs are **800 × 450**, **12 fps** and **8 seconds**, repeating the recorded
excerpt forwards. The videos are **1600 × 900**, **30 fps** and **12 seconds**,
encoded as H.264 MP4 with fast-start playback and no audio. The clips show the
aquarium without its interface controls.

These are recordings of actual procedural 3D animation, rather than generated
video. Spine and fin motion, steering and school separation create the swimming
behavior; this is not a biological or fluid simulation.
